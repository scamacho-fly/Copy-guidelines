# Flybuys Copy Guidelines

## Metadata

- Created: 20 August 2026
- Last Modified: 11 September 2026
- Category: Brand Copy Standards

## Purpose
Provide approved copy standards for all Flybuys customer-facing communications.

## When To Use
- UI copy
- Product messaging
- Marketing copy
- Website content
- App content

## Tone of Voice
- Bright
- Expressive
- Succinct

## Brand Naming
Always write: Flybuys

## Points Language
### Approved
- Earn points
- Score points
- Pack more points
- Stack up on points
- Grow your points pile

### Avoid
- Collect points

## Points Types
### Standard Points
Earn 1 point for every $1 spent at participating partners.

### Bonus Points
Awarded through offers and promotions.

## Redeeming Points
- Money off your shop
- Travel
- Gift cards
- Rewards Store products

## Flybuys Dollars
- 2,000 points = $10 Flybuys Dollars
- Redeemable in $10 increments

## Approved Terminology
- Flybuys points
- Flybuys Dollars
- Bonus points
- Rewards Store
