# Tone of Voice

- Bright: Intelligent and quick-witted.
- Expressive: Conveys thought and feeling.
- Succinct: Punchy and to the point.

---

# Brand Naming

Always write: Flybuys

Rule: Flybuys is one word with a capital F and all remaining letters lowercase.

---

# Points Language

Members:
- Earn points
- Use points
- Redeem rewards

Always say:
- Earn points

You may also say:
- Score points
- Pack more points
- Stack up on points
- Grow your points pile

Never say:
- Collect points

---

# Types of Points

## Standard Points

Earn 1 point for every $1 spent at:
- Bunnings Warehouse
- Coles
- Kmart
- Target
- Officeworks
- Liquorland
- mycar

Partner earn rates may vary.

## Bonus Points

Bonus points come from:
- Offers
- In-store tickets

---

# Referencing Points

Within Flybuys communications use:
- points

When another loyalty currency exists use:
- Flybuys points

## Types of Points Offers

- Points Multiplier Offers (double, triple, 5x, 10x points)
- Bonus Points Offers (250 bonus points, 500 bonus points)
- Points Giveaways (Win a share of 5 million points)

### Bonus Points Terminology

Use:
- bonus points
- bonus Flybuys points

Never use:
- Flybuys bonus points

Flybuys communications:
- Earn 500 bonus points on Uncle Toby's

External communications:
- Earn 500 bonus Flybuys points on Uncle Toby's

---

# Redeeming Points

Members can redeem points for:
- Money off your shop
- Travel
- Gift cards
- Rewards Store products

---

# Gift Cards

- Physical or digital gift cards can only be purchased with points through the Flybuys Rewards Store.
- Gift cards cannot be purchased in-store using Flybuys Dollars.

---

# Flybuys Dollars

## Conversion

- 2,000 points = $10 Flybuys Dollars
- 1 point does not equal 1 Flybuys Dollar

## Rules

- Redeemable only in $10 increments
- Physical Flybuys card required at checkout
- Cannot be used online

## Participating Stores

- Coles
- Coles Express
- Kmart
- Liquorland
- mycar
- Officeworks
- Target

---

# Scanning and Swiping

## Scan

Use when earning points.

Examples:
- Scan or link your Flybuys to earn points.
- Scan your Flybuys in-store or link it online.

## Swipe

Use only when paying with Flybuys Dollars.

Example:
- Swipe your physical Flybuys card through the EFTPOS machine to pay with Flybuys Dollars.

---

# Use of Shop

Use a descriptor before the word shop:

- In-store shop
- Online shop
- Eligible in-store shop
- Qualifying online shop

---

# Present Your Flybuys

Only for Terms & Conditions.

Example:
- To qualify, you must activate this offer before your purchase and present your Flybuys at the checkout.
