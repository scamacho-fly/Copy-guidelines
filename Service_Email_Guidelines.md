# Service Email Guidelines

## Metadata
- Document: Service Email Guidelines
- Last Modified: 11 September 2026
- Category: Customer Communications

## Purpose
Define standards for service, transactional and announcement emails.

## When To Use
- Service updates
- Policy changes
- Account notifications
- Product recalls
- Competition winner notifications

## Email Types
### Marketing Announcements
Used for promotional communications and must include unsubscribe functionality.

### Service Messages
Used for operational, legal and account-related updates.

### Transactional Emails
Triggered by customer actions such as purchases or redemptions.

## Writing Principles
- State intent early.
- Provide context.
- Use clear formatting.
- Follow Flybuys tone of voice.

## Language Standards
### Preferred
- We apologise

### Avoid
- Sorry

## Compliance Requirements
- Legal review required for service communications.
- Do not mix marketing into service messages.
- Comply with privacy and spam obligations.

## Mandatory Recall Elements
- Subject Line
- Pre Header
- H1
- Approved body copy
- Mandatory footer
