# UX/UI Microcopy Guidelines

## Purpose

Create clear, concise and customer-focused copy that helps members understand information, complete tasks and make decisions with confidence.

---

## Core Principles

## Audience Language
Always refer to Flybuys customers as members.
Use Agents only when end user is withing the Service Center

### Preferred
- Member(s)
- Flybuys member(s)
- Agent(s)

Exclusions might be when is a co-branded copy and a partner refers to a mamber as customer.

### Be Clear

Write so members understand the message immediately.
Use familiar language and straightforward sentence structures.

Preferred:
- Activate your offer
- View your rewards

Avoid:
- Initiate offer activation
- Access rewards functionality

---

### Be Concise

Use the fewest words needed to communicate the message.

Preferred:
- Update details
- Earn bonus points

Avoid:
- Please update your personal details below
- You have the opportunity to earn additional bonus points

---

### Be Helpful

Explain what is happening and what members can do next.

Preferred:
- We couldn't load your rewards. Try again.

Avoid:
- Error occurred.

---

### Focus on Customer Value

Lead with benefits and outcomes.

Preferred:
- Save $10 on your next shop.

Avoid:
- Your account is eligible for reward redemption.

---

### Be Human

Write naturally and conversationally.

Preferred:
- You're ready to go.

Avoid:
- Your setup process has been successfully completed.

## Tone of Voice

### Positive

Focus on what members can do rather than restrictions.

### Confident

Use direct language.

### Friendly

Be approachable without being overly casual.

## Writing Standards

### Use Plain English

Avoid technical language, internal terminology and jargon.

### Start With The Action

Preferred:
- Redeem your points
- Update your details
- Activate your offer

### Write For Scanning

Use short sentences, short paragraphs and bullets where appropriate.

## Buttons

Use action-led labels.

Preferred:
- Activate offer
- View rewards
- Redeem points
- Continue
- Update details

## Links

Use descriptive link text.

Preferred:
- View rewards
- Read terms and conditions
- View details

Avoid:
- Click here
- Learn more

## Error Messages

Structure:

Problem + Solution

Example:
- We couldn't update your details. Try again.

## Success Messages

Structure:

Confirmation + Outcome

Example:
- Offer activated.

## Empty States

Structure:

Current State + Next Action

Example:
- No rewards saved yet. Browse rewards to get started.
- Mobile number is required.

## Notifications

Communicate the most important information first.

### Field Labels

Keep labels short and specific.

### Helper Text

Only include when necessary.


## Instructions and Step-by-Step Guidance

Help members complete a task by presenting steps in a logical order.

### Principles

- Keep steps short and action-oriented.
- Start each step with a verb where possible.
- Present one action per step.
- Write in the order members will complete the task.
- Avoid combining multiple actions into a single step.
- Use numbered lists for sequential tasks.
- Clearly explain what happens after an action is completed.

Example:
1. Open the Flybuys app and select Sign in.
2. Select Forgot password.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Select Reset password.
5. Check your email and follow the instructions to create a new password.

Avoid
- Open the Flybuys app, select Sign in, select Forgot password and enter your email address.

## Flybuys Terminology

### Preferred

- Flybuys
- Flybuys points
- Flybuys Dollars
- Bonus points
- Rewards Store

### Points Language

Preferred:
- Earn points
- Score points
- Stack up points
- Pack more points
- Grow your points pile

Avoid:
- Collect points

## Service Communication Principles

- State the purpose immediately.
- Explain what happened.
- Clearly outline next steps.
- Be transparent and factual.
- Avoid promotional language in service messages.

## Accessibility Standards

- Use plain language.
- Avoid unnecessary abbreviations.
- Keep sentences short.
- Use descriptive links and buttons.
- Ensure messages can be understood independently.

## Microcopy Checklist

- Clear and easy to understand.
- Uses approved terminology.
- Focuses on customer value.
- Uses plain English.
- Is concise.
- Includes a clear action.
- Supports accessibility.
- Matches the Flybuys tone of voice.
